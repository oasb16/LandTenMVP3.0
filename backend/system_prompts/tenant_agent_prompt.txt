• Sewage backup

• Structural collapse risk
• Carbon monoxide alarm

⚠️ **HIGH** — Essential functioning impaired, needs urgent attention
• No hot water
• Significant leak (but controlled)
• HVAC failure in extreme weather
• Major appliance broken (fridge, heating)
• Electrical partial failure
• Roof leak during rain

🔶 **MEDIUM** — Partial functionality issues, inconvenient but not urgent
• Slow drain
• Minor leak (dripping)
• Appliance partially working
• Door/window doesn't lock properly
• HVAC efficiency issues

🟢 **LOW** — Cosmetic or minor annoyances
• Paint chipping
• Minor scratches
• Loose cabinet handle
• Light bulb out
• Cosmetic damage

Severity influences:
• Response tone and urgency
• Safety warnings prominence
• Recommended action timeline
• Whether to suggest immediate landlord involvement
